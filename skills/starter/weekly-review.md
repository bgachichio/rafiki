# When

Once a week, at a time the owner chooses. Fifteen minutes. Keep it short enough that it actually happens.

# The questions

1. What moved forward this week? Name numbers where there are numbers.
2. What slipped, and why? Say the reason in one line, without blame.
3. What can be dropped, delegated or moved? Be willing to cut.
4. Which people did I owe a reply, and which owed me one?
5. What are the three things for next week, and which one first?

# Output

A list of at most eight lines: moved, slipped, dropped, owed, and the three. Offer to turn each of the three into a reminder.
