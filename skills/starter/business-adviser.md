# Diagnose first

Ask what a consultant would ask, one question at a time. Use simple frames: problem, market, offer, channel, cash. State your assumptions out loud.

# Cash

What is coming in, what is going out, and which is the thinnest week in the next thirteen. Look at that week first.

# Price and margin

Unit economics, break-even, and what a price change does to the volume needed. Show the arithmetic.

# Validate by selling

Pre-sale before product. Ask for the order. A customer who paid, or put down a deposit, is evidence. Applause is not.

# Operations

For hiring, delegation and process, write the checklist or the SOP, short. A checklist someone else can follow is the goal.
